Our Beginning
2018

Born from a love for extraordinary food.

BiTē Kitchen wasn't built in a boardroom. It started in a small home kitchen in Ernakulam, where our founder Rajan Pillai cooked for neighbours after a long day of dreaming about what food delivery could be — not just convenient, but genuinely beautiful.

"Food is not fuel. It is memory. It is culture. It is the one thing that reminds us we are alive in a particular place, at a particular moment in time."

— Rajan Pillai, Founder

Six years later, BiTē serves over 800 orders a day across Kochi, partnered with farmers within 60km, and employs a team of 12 chefs who each carry a philosophy — that the best meal you've ever had should never be the last.

What We Believe

Three pillars of the BiTē craft.

Not principles we wrote on a wall — convictions we cook by, every single day.

01 🌱
Provenance Matters

Every ingredient carries its geography. We source daily from farms within 60km of our kitchen — not because it's fashionable, but because a tomato grown in Thrissur soil in monsoon season is simply a different ingredient than one that travelled 800km in a cold truck.

02 🔥
Technique is Respect

A perfectly executed dish is an act of respect — for the ingredient, for the tradition, for the person eating it. Our chefs don't cut corners. Stocks are made overnight. Breads are proved for 18 hours. The biryani is still dum-cooked the old way.

03 🫂
Food is Connection

We think about the person on the other end of every order. The student eating alone. The family celebrating. The couple on a Tuesday who just needed something wonderful. Every meal we send out is a small act of hospitality from our kitchen to your table.

Our Journey

Six years, one relentless pursuit.

2018 — The Beginning
BiTē launches from a ghost kitchen in Ernakulam

12 dishes. 3 delivery partners. A borrowed commercial refrigerator. And an unshakeable belief that delivery food could be extraordinary.

2019 — First Recognition
Kerala Food Excellence Award — Best New Restaurant

Less than a year in, the Saffron Biryani earned a write-up in The Hindu Food & Dining. Demand doubled overnight. We ran out of saffron within 48 hours.

2020 — The Pandemic Year
Zero layoffs. Community meal programme launched

When the world stopped, we cooked more. Partnered with three hospitals and two NGOs to deliver 12,000 free meals to frontline workers across Kochi.

2021 — Growing Roots
Farm partnership programme — 8 local farms onboarded

Formalised direct-sourcing relationships with farms across Thrissur, Palakkad and Wayanad. Seasonal menus introduced for the first time.

2022 — Chef's Table Series
Monthly exclusive 10-seat tasting dinners launched

Our most intimate offering — ten guests, one chef, a single evening. Each menu is never repeated. Waitlist now stands at four months.

2024 — Today
120+ dishes. 50,000 customers. Still cooking with love.

Six years on, the same conviction drives every order. The food has evolved. The team has grown. The belief hasn't changed by a single degree.
